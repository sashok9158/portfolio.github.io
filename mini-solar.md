# Mini Solar Car

Currently recruiting a team of students in [CalSol](/calsol.html) to build a mini solar car using simple materials and extra solar cells, with the intention of using it to promote CalSol, utilize resources, and STEM outreach.

### The Current Idea:

- Use popsicle sticks, bottle caps and other simple household items to build the chassis of the car.
- Research and find feasible motors.
- Inventory extra cells in our storage space and test for current output voltage given possible degradation.
- Identify methods of soldering to the cells we have and, depending on motor, if multiple will need to be wired together to meet minimum voltage.
- Build a working prototype by December 2026!

Next steps:

- Controlling the car.
- Scaling the project to various levels including:
    - General: A robust mini car, that can move autonomously. Used for demos, and elementary school outreach
    - Middle School: Scale down to simplest, necessary parts of the car and create a presentation on PV cells, basic circuits and the car so middle school students can build their own car.
    - High School: More in depth presentation, including controls and automation aspects, as well as promotion of the solar car teams and work.
